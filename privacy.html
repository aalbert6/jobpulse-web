Privacy Notice

JobPulse (beta) · Last updated: 2026-07-29

Who we are

JobPulse is a personal project operated by Albert Reina i Buxó, based in Barcelona, Spain, acting as the data controller. Contact: clinch-vassals.3r@icloud.com.

What we collect

Only what we need to match you to jobs: your first name and email; your current role, years of experience and skills; the roles, locations and work styles you're targeting; and optionally a salary floor, deal-breakers, your CV text or LinkedIn URL, and any documents you upload (CV, cover letters, certificates). We do not collect your ID number, date of birth, home address, photo, or any special-category data.

Why, and our legal basis

We use it to generate your personalised weekly job-match email. Our legal basis is your consent, given by ticking the box at sign-up. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who else processes it

We store your profile and any uploaded documents with Supabase, hosted in the EU and encrypted, under a data processing agreement. Generating and emailing your matches is being rolled out; we will name any additional providers here before they process your data. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, and we auto-delete after 12 months of inactivity.

Your rights

At any time, for free, you can access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your data, and withdraw consent. Email clinch-vassals.3r@icloud.com and we'll action it within one month. You can also complain to the Spanish authority, the AEPD.

Security

We encrypt your data in transit and at rest and restrict access to it.
